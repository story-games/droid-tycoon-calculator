Rules

  • The lowest variant wins. Order, low to high: Default, Gold, Diamond, Rainbow, Beskar, Galactic, Stellar = Scourge, Kyber.
  • Scourge ranks with Stellar, below Kyber. On a tie, the leftmost slot wins.
  • Cheapest Scourge: 1 Scourge + 2 Kyber, in any order. With Stellar filler, the Scourge must be to the left of the Stellars.
  • Any variant below Stellar (Default to Galactic) takes over the result.

Source: Droidex fusion guide (fan resource, v1.40.0). Not validated in-game by us.
