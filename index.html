Droid ScourgeRarityType Slot 1 (Scourge Anchor)Slot 2Slot 3

Rules (source: docs/images/scourge-rules.png)

  • Slot 1 = Scourge. Scourge ties with Stellar in rarity weight; the leftmost slot breaks the tie.
  • Slots 2 and 3: only Kyber, Stellar or Scourge. Non-activated Kyber works. Base, Gold, Diamond, Rainbow or Beskar trigger the lowest-tier rule and ruin the Scourge.
  • Ideal: 1 Scourge + 2 Kyber/Stellar. Saves 2/3 of your Scourge stock. 3 Scourge works, but it is wasteful.
